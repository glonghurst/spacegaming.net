    Data product — /data/v1/

    A static tree designed as an API: stable versioned URLs, every payload self-describing (schema_version, generated_at, row_count, license), latest.json alongside immutable dated snapshots, JSONL for bulk and CSV for the daily aggregate. A breaking change ships as /data/v2/ beside it, never in place. None of these paths exist yet — they are shown as the shape to build, which is why nothing above is a link.
